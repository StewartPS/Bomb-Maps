What happened

A tip-and-run raid destroyed Suffolk House in Brassey Road, killing four residents.

Casualty note

Ada Mary Adamson, Jane Charlotte Cana, Edith Lilian Corke and Eleanor May Coventry.

Also worth knowing

53 civilians were killed within Bexhill borough by enemy action during the war — a borough-wide figure. A memorial to them was installed at Marina Court Gardens in 2015.
