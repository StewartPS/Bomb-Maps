What happened

A parachuted land mine detonated at the cathedral's north-east corner, destroying the Ely Chapel — never rebuilt — and the Lady Chapel, and wrecking both organs, the choir stalls, the high altar and every window.

Casualty note

No casualties are recorded at the cathedral. The figure of around 684 killed is the city-wide total for the whole Christmas Blitz.

Also worth knowing

The blast lifted the lead roof clear and dropped it back into place. Because no fire took hold, the building survived where Coventry Cathedral had not. Restoration ran for two decades after the war.
