What happened

The fifteenth-century Guildhall was gutted by fire after incendiaries fell on the civic buildings beside the Ouse. The roof and the medieval stained glass were lost; the shell of the hall survived and was rebuilt to the original design, reopening in 1960.

Also worth knowing

The point here is the Guildhall building itself rather than a plotted bomb position — the bomb census records this quarter of the city by ward, so no single georeferenced strike exists for the hall.
