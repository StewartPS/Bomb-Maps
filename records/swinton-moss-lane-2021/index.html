What happened

A suspected WWII bomb was found in a garden on Moss Lane shortly before half past eleven in the morning. Emergency services stood down and residents returned home by about four in the afternoon.

How it was made safe

Assessed by an Explosive Ordnance Disposal team and declared safe.

Evacuation

Neighbouring homes evacuated behind a cordon.

Also worth knowing

Recorded as a documented response rather than a confirmed munition — not every cordon ends in a detonation.
