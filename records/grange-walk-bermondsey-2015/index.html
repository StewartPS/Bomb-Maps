What happened

A German bomb about five feet long was uncovered on a demolition site at the corner of The Grange and Grange Walk.

How it was made safe

Removed under escort by the Royal Logistic Corps to a quarry in Kent for controlled detonation.

Evacuation

Hundreds of homes evacuated within a 200m cordon, two primary schools closed and around 100 residents rehoused overnight.

Also worth knowing

Reported weights conflict: the BBC gives 250kg, while some contemporary reports described it as roughly 1,000lb.
