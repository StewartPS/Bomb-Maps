Kynaston Road, Orpington — last V-2 death in Britain

What happened

One of the final two V-2 rockets launched at Britain struck Kynaston Road, Orpington, leaving a crater around forty feet across and twenty feet deep.

Casualty note

Ivy Millichamp, aged 34, killed in the kitchen of her home. Twenty-four people were injured.

Also worth knowing

Ivy Millichamp was the last civilian killed by enemy action in Britain during the Second World War.
