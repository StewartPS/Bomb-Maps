What happened

Two parachute mines fell on Heavitree, causing damage across more than thirty streets including Woodstock Road and Cranbrook Road.

Casualty note

Four killed and 26 injured.

Also worth knowing

The most destructive attack on Exeter before the 1942 Baedeker raids.
