What happened

Two waves of bombers attacked in clear conditions, preceded by parachute flares, striking Culverland Road, Cumberland Terrace, Morgan Square, King Street, Fore Street and Paris Street.

Casualty note

73 killed, with 20 seriously and 53 slightly injured.

Also worth knowing

The deadliest of the April Baedeker attacks on Exeter and the second-deadliest raid on the city, after 4 May.
