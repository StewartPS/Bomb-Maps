What happened

Serious damage was inflicted on East Crawford Street and Belville Street on the first night of the Greenock Blitz.

Also worth knowing

The coordinate is a postcode centroid (PA15 2EA), not a surveyed site location. No per-site casualty figure is published; the town-wide toll of 271 is carried on the area record.
