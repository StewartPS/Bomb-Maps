What happened

A 1,000kg high-explosive bomb struck Burgess Street, devastating King Edward Street, Victoria Street, Grime Street and Fotherby Street. Air raid warden John Denford and sailor Fred Dolphin rescued a young woman from a collapsed terraced house on King Edward Street.

Also worth knowing

Part of the wider Grimsby & Cleethorpes butterfly bomb raid of 13–14 June 1943 — see the area record for the full raid and its casualty total. No per-site death toll is published for this street cluster specifically.
