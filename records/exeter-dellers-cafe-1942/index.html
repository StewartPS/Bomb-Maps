What happened

Deller's Cafe, a landmark galleried tea room and one of the best-known social venues in the city, was burnt out during the Baedeker raid.

Also worth knowing

Its loss is still cited as one of the most-mourned casualties of the Exeter Blitz — a reminder that what a city misses is not always what was most valuable.
