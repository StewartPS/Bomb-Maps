What happened

A direct hit on 60 Bedford Place killed the Cox family of four during the 21 April 1943 raid.

Casualty note

The Cox family killed: mother Williamina, 22, and three children. Named-victim detail from a single source, not yet cross-checked against a second — treat with the same caution as the area record's casualty note.
