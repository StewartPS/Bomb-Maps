What happened

Incendiaries fell across the east of the city — Regent Street, Tang Hall, the Foss Islands goods station and Lawrence Street — with a direct hit on number 12 Belle Vue Terrace.

Casualty note

Three people died in this raid across the city: Civil Defence Warden Joseph Kirby, 57; Percy Beckwith, 61, at St Aelred's rest centre; and Thomas Marsh, 84, of Aldwark. None of the three is recorded at this address, so no figure is placed on this pin.
