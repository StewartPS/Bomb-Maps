What happened

A lone raider followed a train into Redruth and bombed the station area and town centre, dropping eleven bombs on Bassett Street, Bond Street, Wesley Street and West Trewirgie.

Casualty note

Six killed and twelve injured, five of them seriously.

Also worth knowing

One of the eleven bombs, at East End, failed to explode.
