Carlton Cinema, Taunton Road, Sparkbrook

25 October 1940 · Birmingham, West Midlands

What happened

A high-explosive bomb tore through the roof and burst in the auditorium a few yards in front of the screen during a showing of the Dorothy Lamour film Typhoon. The entrance hall was turned into an emergency first-aid post.

Casualty note

Nineteen in the audience, the heaviest losses among those seated nearest the screen.
