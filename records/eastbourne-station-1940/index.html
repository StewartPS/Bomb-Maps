What happened

A bomb hit a train at Eastbourne railway station, one of many raids on what the Home Office designated the most raided town in the South East region.

Casualty note

Four killed that day when a bomb struck a train standing in the station.

Also worth knowing

Town-wide totals vary widely and should be treated with care: 187 civilians are recorded as dying in the borough through enemy action, a Civil Defence regional officer cited 96 enemy air attacks — more than any other town in the region — and one oral-history account says over 400 bombs fell between July 1940 and December 1941.
