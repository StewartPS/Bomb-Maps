What happened

During the same raid Park Street was severely burnt, with a contemporary journalist recording that every third shop was ablaze. Bristol Museum & Art Gallery on Queen's Road was hit, and the university district and Clifton Parish Church were damaged.

Also worth knowing

No separate casualty figure is published for Park Street. The 207 dead recorded for the night of 24 November are a city-wide total and are not broken down by street.
