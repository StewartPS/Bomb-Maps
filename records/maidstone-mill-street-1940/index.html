What happened

A lone Dornier 17 bombed Mill Street shortly before nine in the morning — the last bombs to fall on the town during the Battle of Britain period. The intended target was the Rootes Motors repair workshops, which built and repaired military vehicles.

Casualty note

Deaths occurred but no figure is given in the source consulted, so none is asserted here.

Also worth knowing

Shops and offices in Mill Street were heavily damaged and the town's trolleybus system had to be re-routed.
