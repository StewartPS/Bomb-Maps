What happened

A 50kg German bomb was found on a building site roughly 200 metres from Wembley Stadium. The Army said the device posed a genuine risk to life.

How it was made safe

Removed by the Army and detonated in Bedfordshire.

Evacuation

About 300 properties evacuated, including television studios then in use, with around 70 people spending the night in an evacuation centre.
