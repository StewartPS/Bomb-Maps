What happened

The central shopping and civic areas were devastated during the seven heaviest nights of the Blitz, part of a citywide toll of 1,174 civilians killed, 3,209 injured, and over 4,000 properties destroyed outright.

Citywide Blitz toll, not specific to this area alone.
