What happened

Further bombs from the same 28 April 1941 raid killed Walter Eales at Glebe Terrace and William Rogers at Belle Vue Road.

Also worth knowing

Part of the same short, deadly raid described in the Fore Street & Commercial Place record — nine Saltash civilians were killed across the town in a single 25-minute spell.
