What happened

Bombs fell on the centre of Salcombe overnight. RAF 266 (Rhodesia) Squadron, then tasked with stopping the hit-and-run raids, shot down one Focke-Wulf 190 over nearby Chivelstone and a second as it reached the French coast — but others got through.

Casualty note

Deaths at 65A and 9 Fore Street and at 1 Clifton Place, including a mother and her 15-year-old daughter.

Also worth knowing

Among the dead were a Fire Guard and a member of the St John Ambulance Brigade.
