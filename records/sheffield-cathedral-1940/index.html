What happened

Bombs falling on Campo Lane and Vicar Lane demolished the west end of Sheffield Cathedral early in the first night of the Blitz, at around half past nine.

Casualty note

None reliably stated for this site.

Also worth knowing

Sheffield's war total was over 660 killed, more than 1,500 injured and 40,000 made homeless — city-wide figures across the whole campaign, not for this building.
