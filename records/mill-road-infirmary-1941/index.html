What happened

A direct hit destroyed three ward blocks of Mill Road Infirmary, including the maternity ward, killing mothers, newborn babies and medical staff. Surviving patients were transferred to Broadgreen Hospital.

Casualty note

Approximately 80. The figure is an estimate rather than a confirmed count, and the source notes the true number is likely higher.

Also worth knowing

Superintendent Leonard Findlay received the George Medal and Matron Gertrude Riding the OBE for their conduct that night. The hospital site is now an industrial estate.
