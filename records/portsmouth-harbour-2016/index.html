What happened

A 500lb German bomb was found in Portsmouth Harbour early one morning, prompting a 500m exclusion zone across the waterfront.

How it was made safe

Towed out and detonated about 1.5 miles east of the Isle of Wight.

Evacuation

Gunwharf Quays, Old Portsmouth, the Historic Dockyard, Portsmouth Harbour station and the Gosport and Wightlink ferry terminals all cleared.

Also worth knowing

The Royal Navy noted that such devices still pose a very serious threat when they are moved — the reason a find that has lain harmlessly for eighty years still closes a city centre.
