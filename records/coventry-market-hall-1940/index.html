What happened

The raid destroyed the central library, the Market Hall, hundreds of shops and public buildings, and the sixteenth-century Palace Yard. The Market Hall's clock tower was left standing amid the ruins and survives.

Casualty note

Not separately recorded. Do not read the city-wide 568 as applying here.
