What happened

A daylight raid on Falmouth Docks set the 7,000-ton motor vessel British Chancellor ablaze and killed ten sailors and dock workers. It was among the first significant attacks on a Cornish target and one of the opening actions of what became the Battle of Britain.

Casualty note

Ten sailors and dock staff killed.

Also worth knowing

Falmouth's deep natural harbour and ship-repair yards made it the most heavily targeted place in Cornwall throughout the war.
