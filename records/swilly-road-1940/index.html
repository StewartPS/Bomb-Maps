What happened

The first bombs to fall on Plymouth landed just before midday on a block of eight Corporation houses in Swilly Road, destroying three and killing a 33-year-old woman, a 58-year-old man, and a 13-year-old boy — the city's first civilian casualties of the war.

Also worth knowing

This is the opening entry in the Town Clerk's wartime 'bomb book', now held at The Box. Note: some secondary sources (citing Gill, 1993) place this first raid in the neighbouring North Prospect area rather than Swilly Road by name — the two are adjoining streets in the same St Budeaux/Swilly district, and casualty/date details agree.
