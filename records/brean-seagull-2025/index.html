What happened

On Bank Holiday Monday a metal-detector user found a cylinder-shaped object on Brean beach behind the Seagull pub. Coastguards and police cordoned off the area and a bomb disposal team attended.

How it was made safe

Assessed by a bomb disposal team and declared harmless.

Also worth knowing

Recorded as a documented response rather than a confirmed munition — the object was ultimately assessed as safe. Not every cordon ends in a detonation, and the ones that do not are part of the picture too.
