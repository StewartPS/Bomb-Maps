Civic Centre Art Gallery school shelter

6 November 1940 · Southampton, Hampshire

View this record on the map →

6 November 1940
Record type

Bomb weight

Location

Position accuracy
Confirmed site, building-level

What happened

A 500lb bomb fell through the roof of the art gallery in a daylight raid and exploded in the lower floors, where a school class was sheltering during an art lesson.

Casualty note

35 killed including 14 or 15 children. Of the fifteen children sheltering in the basement, only one survived.
