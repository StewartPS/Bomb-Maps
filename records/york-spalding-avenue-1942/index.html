What happened

Ten people were killed in this suburban street on the northern edge of the city, eight of them at number 110 — members of the Helstrip, Duncan and Hyde families, four of whom were children aged between ten months and six years.

Casualty note

Ten named individuals recorded in Spalding Avenue in the Raids Over York casualty register.

Also worth knowing

Spalding Avenue rarely appears in popular accounts of the raid, which tend to follow the Guildhall, the station and the Bar Convent. It was one of the worst domestic losses of the night.
