What happened

A Junkers Ju 88 bombed the boys' school at Petworth, killing around thirty-two people, most of them pupils.

Casualty note

Around 32 killed, including 28 boys.

Also worth knowing

One of the most devastating single incidents of the war in rural West Sussex, commemorated by a memorial cross registered with the Imperial War Museum.
