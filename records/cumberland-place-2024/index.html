What happened

Suspected unexploded ordnance was found near Cumberland Place in the city centre shortly after 10am. Military bomb disposal experts attended and closures were lifted by early afternoon.

Evacuation

Office workers and gym users evacuated; cordons placed at Commercial Road and Watts Park.
