What happened

The school was in use as an Auxiliary Fire Service sub-station during the heavy raid on the night of 19-20 April 1941. A parachute mine struck the building, destroying it and killing everyone inside.

Casualty note

Thirteen from the London Fire Brigade and Auxiliary Fire Service in London, and twenty-one from AFS Beckenham who had come in to help. Among them were firewomen, messengers and a despatch rider.

Also worth knowing

The largest single loss of fire brigade personnel in English history. Lansbury Lawrence Primary School now stands on the site and carries a memorial plaque. Recorded here after a submitted dataset placed a 240-death incident at Waterloo Bridge on this same night — no such incident could be corroborated, and this appears to be the raid it was reaching for.
