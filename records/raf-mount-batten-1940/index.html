What happened

Bomb damage to buildings at the RAF flying-boat station on Mount Batten during Raid 22 of the Blitz.

Also worth knowing

Sourced from Plymouth Archives catalogue entries (refs 3488/5458 and 3488/5463) — captions only. The photographs themselves have not been viewed, so casualties and the specific buildings hit are not yet confirmed.
