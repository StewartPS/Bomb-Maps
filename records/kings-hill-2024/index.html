What happened

A 50kg German unexploded bomb was uncovered during construction work at King's Hill, on the site of the former RAF West Malling airfield, which was heavily attacked during the Battle of Britain.

How it was made safe

Destroyed in a controlled explosion the following day, behind blast mitigation installed by an EOD team.

Evacuation

A school and several neighbouring premises evacuated behind a police cordon.

Also worth knowing

A second 50kg German bomb had been found and destroyed at the same site in June 2023. Wartime airfields remain among the most reliable places to find unexploded ordnance eighty years on.
