National Picture Theatre, Beverley Road

18 March 1941 · Hull, East Riding of Yorkshire

What happened

The cinema was wrecked during a raid while around 150 people sheltered in its foyer. None were killed.

Casualty note

About 150 people were sheltering in the foyer and all escaped.

Also worth knowing

The surviving facade and fragments of foyer and vestibule were Grade II listed in 2007 as the last substantial civilian bomb ruin still standing in Britain. Restoration began in 2024.
