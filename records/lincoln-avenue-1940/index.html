What happened

Plymouth Police photographed an incendiary bomb at Lincoln Avenue during Raid 25 of the Blitz.

Also worth knowing

Sourced from a Plymouth Archives catalogue entry (ref AD7/13/3) — a caption only. The photograph itself has not been viewed, so casualties and the exact address are not yet confirmed.
