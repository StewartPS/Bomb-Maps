What happened

At 7.21am a V-2 struck Hughes Mansions on Vallance Road, destroying the central block of the flats and killing 134 men, women and children, many of them from the local Jewish community. It was the last V-2 to fall on London.

Also worth knowing

A memorial plaque in the rear courtyard records the names of the dead.
