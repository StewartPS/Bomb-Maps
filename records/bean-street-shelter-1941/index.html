What happened

During the March 1941 raids on the Hessle Road and St Andrew's Dock district, a parachute mine struck a public air-raid shelter in Bean Street.

Casualty note

Deliberately left blank. Sources record that the shelter was hit and that there were multiple deaths, but give no figure, and none is invented here.
