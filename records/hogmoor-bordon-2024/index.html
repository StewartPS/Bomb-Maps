What happened

Construction workers redeveloping the former army training area found a German WWII bomb at Hogmoor Inclosure. The controlled explosion was heard up to fifteen miles away.

How it was made safe

Destroyed in a controlled explosion behind a blast wall built from more than 300 tonnes of imported sand.

Evacuation

Properties evacuated and the gas supply cut before detonation.
