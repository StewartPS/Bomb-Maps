What happened

Two German fighters attacked St Ives, opening with machine-gun fire across Porthminster Beach before strafing the main streets and heading back out to sea. One woman was killed.

Casualty note

One woman killed.

Also worth knowing

Part of a run of tip-and-run attacks across Cornwall in August 1942; reported in The Cornishman on 3 September 1942.
