What happened

Bombs struck the Shoreham Shipping Coal Company wharf in the harbour.

Casualty note

Five killed, including 17-year-old Home Guard member George Earthey.

Also worth knowing

Thirty-seven raids were made on Shoreham and Southwick during the war, causing 17 deaths and 108 injuries, with over 100 high-explosive bombs and more than 2,000 incendiaries recorded — town-wide figures.
