What happened

Bombing struck the residential streets of Alma Terrace and St James Street, killing nine people and injuring 26. It was the deadliest single attack on Penzance in the whole war.

Casualty note

Nine killed and 26 injured; hundreds of houses damaged.

Also worth knowing

Across the war Penzance recorded 16 dead, 48 houses completely destroyed and 157 seriously damaged, under more than 700 air raid alerts.
