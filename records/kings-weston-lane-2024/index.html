What happened

A suspected unexploded device was uncovered during construction work on Kings Weston Lane in Avonmouth. Police established a safety cordon and bomb disposal specialists attended to examine the item before the road reopened.

Also worth knowing

The device type and final disposal were not published. The month is taken from a UXO contractor's incident list — the BBC report itself carries no visible date.
