What happened

The Metropolitan-Vickers works on Mosley Road, one of the largest engineering plants in the Trafford Park industrial estate, was badly damaged during the Christmas Blitz.

Casualty note

No site-specific figure available.

Also worth knowing

Trafford Park was the Luftwaffe's principal industrial objective in Manchester, also housing Ford's aero-engine plant and A. V. Roe production. 272 tons of high explosive fell on the city on the night of 22/23 December and a further 195 tons the next night — city-wide figures.
