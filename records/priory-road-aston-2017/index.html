What happened

A 250kg German SC250 bomb, containing 139kg of high explosive, was found on a construction site off Priory Road at about 9.45 on a Monday morning.

How it was made safe

Banked with roughly 250 tonnes of sand and destroyed in a controlled detonation the following afternoon.

Evacuation

Around 100 homes and businesses evacuated, about 80 people spending the night in rest centres; the A38(M) Aston Expressway and stretches of the M6 were closed.
