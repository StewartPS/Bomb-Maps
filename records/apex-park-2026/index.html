What happened

Construction contractors at Apex Park found small pieces of suspected unexploded ordnance on the east side of the lake, including what appeared to be mortar shells and a grenade. Somerset Council imposed a 50-metre cordon, halted the works and put up temporary fencing.

Also worth knowing

A specialist contractor was to be engaged to assess and clear any remaining ordnance. The council notice was updated on 6 March 2026; the exact discovery date was not stated.
